# RealReach — Full Product Context Handoff

Use this as the current authoritative product context for RealReach.

This document captures the original idea, the problems identified during discussion and research, the pivots that followed, and the current version of the product that has emerged.

The goal is not to blindly reproduce the first pitch deck. The concept has evolved significantly since then.

---

## 1. Product Origin

RealReach started from a simple observation about social-media boosting.

Businesses can buy followers from existing follower-selling platforms, but those followers are usually bots or low-quality accounts.

The result is obvious:

- A page may have 100,000 followers but only 100 likes.
- Comments are missing or obviously fake.
- The follower count does not translate into real attention.
- The artificial audience can actually reduce trust once people notice the mismatch.
- Bots can never become customers.

The original RealReach idea was therefore:

> Businesses pay RealReach, and RealReach pays real people with genuine social-media accounts to follow, like, comment, repost or otherwise engage with those businesses.

The user receives a small amount of money for completing the action.

The business pays considerably more than it would for bot followers because the engagement comes from actual human beings.

The initial value proposition was essentially:

> Real Nigerians. Real engagement. Real potential customers.

---

# 2. The Original Two-Sided Marketplace

There are two primary sides.

### Businesses

Businesses want:

- followers
- likes
- comments
- reposts/shares
- exposure
- social proof
- potentially customers

They create and fund campaigns.

### Earners / Users

Real people register their social accounts and earn small amounts of money for completing campaign tasks.

For example:

> Follow this Instagram account and earn ₦100.

The amount is deliberately not intended to become someone's salary.

The original thinking was that there are millions of Nigerians who already have:

- smartphones
- mobile data
- Instagram/TikTok/Facebook/X accounts
- spare time

and would gladly complete simple tasks for small rewards.

Existing Nigerian microtask platforms later confirmed that this behaviour already exists.

---

# 3. Market Research Changed One Important Assumption

Research found that RealReach is NOT the first Nigerian platform to pay humans for social engagement.

Existing examples include businesses such as:

- Hawkit
- GoJelly
- TaskletPay
- RepliCash

These platforms already allow Nigerians to earn money from activities such as:

- following accounts
- liking content
- commenting
- reposting
- completing other digital microtasks

This validates the demand from the user side.

RealReach therefore should NOT assume its moat is simply:

> “Unlike follower sellers, ours are real Nigerians.”

That already exists.

The competitive question became:

> Why should a business use RealReach instead of an existing microtask or social-boosting platform?

That question drove most of the later product evolution.

---

# 4. Critical Product Insight: Real Human ≠ Interested Human

The first major pivot came from recognizing this distinction.

Suppose someone is paid ₦100 to follow a wig company.

That person:

- is real
- owns a real account
- genuinely performed the follow

But they may still have absolutely no interest in wigs.

Therefore:

> Real engagement does not automatically equal relevant engagement.

There is an important hierarchy:

**Bot  
< Random paid human  
< Relevant paid human  
< Organically interested human**

RealReach cannot realistically promise the fourth category.

The product becomes much stronger if it can increasingly deliver the third.

---

# 5. Current RealReach Definition

The current concept is:

> **RealReach is a two-sided marketplace where businesses pay for verified exposure and engagement from real Nigerians, while users earn money for performing genuine social actions. The platform begins with affordable general reach for businesses and progressively offers more valuable targeted and qualified audiences as it learns which users are actually relevant to which businesses.**

RealReach should NOT be understood as only a follower-selling website.

The broader idea is an **audience marketplace**.

Businesses are buying different qualities of human attention.

Users themselves also become more valuable as RealReach learns more about them.

---

# 6. RealReach Does NOT Guarantee Sales

This distinction is important.

RealReach does not say:

> Buy 500 users and receive X sales.

The basic product provides **potential exposure to real humans**.

Jordan's intended distinction is:

> They are not automatically leads. They are people capable of becoming interested.

Only when RealReach has additional evidence of interest should someone be classified as a stronger prospect or lead.

Therefore the product develops through progressively stronger audience types.

---

# 7. Current Product Levels

A useful way to understand the current business is through three levels.

## Level 1 — General Reach

This is the simplest and cheapest service.

A business purchases actions from genuine users without claiming that those users have any specific existing interest in the business.

Examples:

- 500 real Instagram followers
- 300 likes
- 100 comments
- reposts
- story interactions
- other supported engagement

This is primarily valuable for:

- small businesses
- vendors
- new brands
- creators
- businesses that mainly want visibility/social proof

Example:

A small cake vendor with 200 followers may simply want the page to look alive and reach more genuine Nigerian users.

They may not require sophisticated lead generation.

The value is:

> Real people instead of bots.

This remains a legitimate product.

---

# 8. Level 2 — Targeted Reach

Businesses pay more to reach people RealReach believes are relevant.

Example:

Instead of giving a Lagos gym 1,000 random Nigerians, RealReach could give the gym:

- users in Lagos
- within a useful demographic
- with demonstrated fitness/sports/gym interest
- with reliable RealReach histories

At this level the users are still not guaranteed leads.

They are simply much better potential customers than random people.

The business is paying for:

> More relevant human attention.

---

# 9. Level 3 — Qualified / Curated Audiences

Over time, certain RealReach users can become significantly more commercially valuable.

For example, someone may repeatedly:

- complete gaming-related campaigns
- click gaming businesses
- answer gaming surveys
- show interest in gaming products
- engage with gaming offers
- voluntarily request gaming opportunities

RealReach can eventually infer that the person has genuine demonstrated gaming interest.

The platform can then offer premium campaigns where businesses pay considerably more for access to users with stronger evidence of interest.

Eventually an even stronger user may explicitly:

- request information
- submit an enquiry
- complete a lead form
- click through to WhatsApp
- request an offer
- demonstrate buying intent

At that point the person can become an actual qualified lead rather than merely a follower.

Therefore RealReach's product ladder can roughly be thought of as:

**Real human  
→ Relevant human  
→ Demonstrated-interest human  
→ Qualified lead**

Businesses pay progressively more as audience quality increases.

---

# 10. Users Increase in Value Over Time

A central part of the current concept is that users are not interchangeable forever.

A brand-new RealReach account is relatively unknown.

Over time RealReach can learn:

- how reliable the user is
- which categories interest them
- what kinds of campaigns they accept
- what campaigns they reject
- whether they remain engaged
- whether they frequently reverse actions
- whether their account behaves naturally
- whether their submitted interests match their behaviour
- how saturated they are with campaigns

A user with several months of reliable activity should therefore be more valuable than someone who signed up five minutes ago.

---

# 11. Do Not Blindly Trust Signup Interests

A major discussion concerned how RealReach discovers what someone actually likes.

Simply asking during onboarding:

> What are your interests?

is not enough.

RealReach is a money-earning platform.

Some users will choose whatever categories they believe provide more tasks or money.

Therefore self-reported interests should only be considered an initial weak signal.

For example:

A user may select:

- fitness
- gaming
- technology
- fashion

But RealReach should gradually learn from behaviour.

If the user repeatedly:

- accepts gaming tasks
- completes gaming surveys
- clicks gaming businesses
- maintains gaming-related engagement
- ignores fashion campaigns

then gaming should become a stronger inferred interest regardless of what the signup form originally said.

The principle is:

> **Ask users what they like, but eventually trust demonstrated behaviour more than declarations.**

This does NOT require artificial intelligence during the earliest version.

It is primarily a product concept.

---

# 12. Reliability and Interest Are Different

Another important distinction:

Someone can be extremely reliable without being commercially valuable for every business.

Example:

User A completes absolutely every task and maintains follows well.

That person may have very high reliability.

But they may be following businesses purely for money.

User B may be slightly less reliable overall but have strong demonstrated fitness interest.

For a fitness business, User B may be considerably more valuable.

Therefore conceptually RealReach should distinguish between things such as:

### Reliability

> Does this person consistently do what they agreed to do?

and:

### Relevance / Interest

> Is this person actually a good audience for this business?

These are not the same thing.

---

# 13. The Adversarial User Assumption

One of the biggest disagreements in the original discussion was user behaviour.

The initial assumption was roughly:

> Poor users will value the future earning opportunity enough that many will remain following businesses long-term.

This should NOT be the foundation of the product.

RealReach should assume that users will try to maximize earnings while minimizing effort.

The expected behaviour should be:

> Follow → collect reward → unfollow → find another task.

Or more generally:

> “How much money can I extract from this system while doing the minimum required?”

This is not because every user is malicious.

It is simply the safest way to design a marketplace involving financial incentives.

The important principle is:

> **If RealReach remains useful and economically viable even when people try to game it, then the business is much stronger.**

---

# 14. Retention Rewards Remain Useful

Jordan had already proposed:

- long-term rewards
- account tiers
- reliability rewards
- increased earnings for reliable users
- referral incentives

Those ideas remain valid.

The research did NOT invalidate them.

What changed was the assumption that they completely solve churn.

They should instead reduce churn and reward quality.

For example:

A long-term reliable user may:

- access better campaigns
- receive larger rewards
- receive more tasks
- gain higher status
- gain access to premium campaigns

The system makes reliability financially rational.

---

# 15. Churn Is Expected

RealReach should NOT claim:

> Human followers never unfollow.

Humans naturally change behaviour.

Even ordinary follower sellers lose followers.

The objective is not zero churn.

The objective is:

- understand churn
- measure it
- minimize abusive churn
- reward better retention
- account for churn in pricing
- make reliable users more valuable

Therefore a useful RealReach metric may eventually be:

> 30-day retention

rather than:

> Permanent followers.

---

# 16. Do Not Create Unsustainable Retention Economics

Another problem identified was excessive delayed payouts.

Example:

- ₦100 paid immediately
- ₦300 after 30 days

For 1,000 people this alone would create:

> ₦400,000 in user payouts

before considering:

- RealReach margin
- payment costs
- fraud
- customer acquisition
- operations

Therefore RealReach should be careful about repeatedly paying users merely to remain followers.

Long-term rewards should increasingly correspond to continued useful behaviour rather than passive existence.

Possible valuable behaviours include:

- meaningful campaign participation
- relevant engagement
- answering surveys
- keeping strong reliability
- completing higher-value tasks
- responding to relevant business opportunities
- referrals
- providing useful campaign feedback

The business must remain economically sensible.

---

# 17. Audience Recycling / Saturation Is a Major Structural Risk

A later discussion identified another important problem.

Imagine RealReach eventually has:

- 1 million active users
- 5,000 businesses

If every business eventually receives the same pool of 1 million users, those users could end up following hundreds or thousands of businesses.

Technically:

- the followers are still real
- the accounts are still human
- the follower counts still exist

But attention becomes diluted.

A user following thousands of RealReach businesses cannot meaningfully see or care about everything in their feed.

Therefore:

> **Real followers do not automatically equal real reach.**

This creates different rules for different product levels.

---

# 18. Saturation Is Less Dangerous for General Reach

For the basic General Reach product, some recycling is acceptable.

The business understands that it is primarily buying:

- real follower count
- genuine human accounts
- social proof
- some exposure

There is no guarantee that every follower is deeply interested.

A small business may still find this useful.

---

# 19. Saturation Matters Much More for Targeted Reach

Once RealReach charges businesses more for:

- relevance
- attention
- potential customer value
- qualified audiences

the same users cannot endlessly be assigned to every business.

RealReach should conceptually account for things such as:

- how many campaigns a user has received
- how many businesses they already followed through RealReach
- how many campaigns in the same category they recently completed
- how frequently they are being exposed to commercial tasks
- how saturated they are

A person who has followed 100 cake vendors is probably not a premium candidate for cake vendor number 101.

Therefore the audience itself is a scarce resource.

RealReach should protect that resource more aggressively as campaign quality increases.

---

# 20. Small Businesses Remain an Important Market

The move toward targeting should NOT accidentally turn RealReach into a product only for large corporations.

The whole market remains relevant.

A small Instagram vendor may genuinely only want:

> Give my business 500 real followers and some likes/comments.

That customer does not need an advanced lead-generation platform.

RealReach can serve them through General Reach.

A larger business may want:

> Show this campaign to 10,000 Lagos users aged 18–30 with demonstrated fitness interest.

That business purchases Targeted Reach.

Another company may want:

> Give us people who have specifically shown intent to purchase fitness equipment.

That becomes a premium qualified-audience product.

Same marketplace.

Different audience quality.

Different pricing.

---

# 21. The Current Strategic Positioning

The strongest long-term interpretation of RealReach is:

> **Businesses pay to be discovered by relevant real Nigerians, while Nigerians earn for verified attention and qualified actions.**

This is broader and more defensible than:

> Buy followers from Nigerians.

Follower campaigns can still be a major starting product.

They just do not have to define the entire company forever.

The long-term concept is closer to a:

> Human attention / audience marketplace.

---

# 22. Social Platform Reality — Extremely Important

RealReach must NOT be designed around imaginary API capabilities.

Research was conducted into Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and X.

There is NO universal API where RealReach can simply tell Instagram:

> Send this business 500 followers.

The actual follower is a human user.

The human generally has to perform the action inside the social-media platform.

RealReach orchestrates:

- discovery
- assignment
- incentives
- verification
- payments
- matching
- analytics

The social network itself remains where the social action occurs.

---

# 23. General RealReach Social Flow

The intended conceptual flow is:

**Business  
→ RealReach campaign  
→ RealReach matches eligible user  
→ User opens social platform  
→ User manually completes action  
→ RealReach verifies action where possible  
→ User receives reward  
→ Business receives campaign result**

RealReach should never depend on:

> Business → RealReach → RealReach magically creates social followers.

That capability generally does not exist.

---

# 24. Instagram API Reality

Instagram's APIs provide useful functionality, especially around professional/business/creator accounts.

However they should NOT be assumed to provide unrestricted visibility into arbitrary normal users.

In particular RealReach should not assume it can universally ask Instagram:

> Does User X currently follow Business Y?

for every normal Instagram user and business.

Some engagement types are easier to verify than others.

For example, if a professional Instagram business connects its account, comments on business media can potentially be much easier to observe through official platform integrations.

Therefore:

> Not every Instagram task will have the same verification quality.

---

# 25. TikTok API Reality

TikTok similarly allows useful account connection and profile information through official APIs.

Useful data may include things such as:

- account identity
- public profile information
- follower counts
- following counts
- video information

However the ability to freely inspect complete follower/following relationships is not generally available to ordinary commercial apps.

TikTok has deeper research APIs, but those are intended for qualifying research use and should NOT be treated as a commercial RealReach dependency.

Therefore RealReach should never depend on unrestricted TikTok social-graph access.

---

# 26. Facebook Reality

Facebook provides APIs around:

- Pages
- businesses
- Page engagement
- managed business assets
- insights

but should similarly not be treated as giving RealReach unrestricted access to normal people's personal social graphs.

---

# 27. X Is More API-Friendly

X currently provides comparatively stronger follower/following APIs.

However RealReach should still not design its entire architecture around the assumption that every social network will eventually behave like X.

The product should remain useful even where social APIs are weak.

---

# 28. Account Connection Is Still Useful

Even if a platform cannot tell RealReach every account someone follows, connecting social accounts can still provide significant value.

Depending on the platform and available permissions, RealReach may learn things such as:

- social identity
- username
- account existence
- profile information
- account statistics
- connected business media
- engagement events
- account quality indicators

This helps determine whether someone is:

- genuine
- active
- established
- suspicious
- likely to be a throwaway account

But:

> **Connecting a social account does not mean RealReach has unrestricted surveillance over it.**

---

# 29. Verification Must Be Hybrid

Because social APIs have limitations, RealReach's verification system should conceptually allow multiple methods.

Depending on the task and platform, verification may involve:

- official APIs
- OAuth-connected account information
- webhooks
- screenshot proof
- manual moderation
- automated evidence checks
- random audits
- business confirmation
- behavioural trust/reputation

Different campaign types may therefore have different verification confidence.

The product should be honest about this internally.

---

# 30. Screenshots May Be Necessary Initially

Existing Nigerian platforms already use screenshot-based task verification.

RealReach may need this for certain early campaign types where official APIs cannot directly prove the action.

Example flow:

1. User receives task.
2. User opens Instagram/TikTok/etc.
3. User performs action.
4. User returns to RealReach.
5. User submits requested proof.
6. Proof is checked.
7. Task is approved or rejected.
8. Reward becomes eligible.

Over time RealReach should automate verification where reliable official platform capabilities exist.

The MVP should NOT delay launch waiting for universal automated verification that may never exist.

---

# 31. RealReach Behaviour Is Its Own Dataset

A major strategic insight is that RealReach should not depend on Instagram or TikTok to tell it what every user likes.

RealReach itself will accumulate behavioural data.

For example:

A user:

- accepts 20 gaming campaigns
- accepts 15 tech campaigns
- ignores most beauty campaigns
- consistently engages with football campaigns
- completes gaming surveys
- repeatedly clicks gaming business offers

That information belongs to RealReach.

It becomes the foundation of audience categorisation.

This is important because it makes the company's long-term targeting capability partly independent of social-platform APIs.

# 32. Business Flow

A business should conceptually experience something like this.

### Business joins RealReach

Provides its identity and relevant business information.

### Business chooses what it wants

Examples:

- Instagram followers
- likes
- comments
- reposts
- visibility
- targeted exposure
- other supported actions

### Business chooses audience quality

For example:

**General**

Real humans without strong targeting.

**Targeted**

Users matching specified characteristics/interests.

Later:

**Qualified**

Users with demonstrated interest or commercial intent.

### Business sets campaign size/budget

Example:

> 500 users.

### Business funds campaign

RealReach holds the campaign value and distributes tasks.

### Campaign is distributed

Eligible users receive or discover campaign opportunities.

### Users complete actions

Social action normally occurs on the external platform.

### RealReach verifies results

Using whatever verification method is valid for that particular action/platform.

### Business sees results

At minimum:

- requested quantity
- completed quantity
- verified quantity
- pending quantity

Over time stronger analytics can include:

- retention
- audience quality
- location
- interests
- interaction rates
- clicks
- enquiries
- conversions where measurable

### Business decides whether to run another campaign

Repeat purchase is one of the most important signals that RealReach is actually providing value.

---

# 33. User / Earner Flow

A RealReach user should conceptually experience:

### Signup

The user creates a RealReach account.

### Social account connection/verification

They provide the social accounts they intend to use.

Potential networks include:

- Instagram
- TikTok
- Facebook
- X

The exact platforms supported can expand progressively.

### Initial profile

User provides useful information such as:

- location
- basic demographics where appropriate
- initial interests

These are initial signals rather than unquestionable truth.

### Tasks become available

Example:

> Follow this business and earn ₦X.

or:

> Like this post.

or:

> Comment on this campaign.

or eventually:

> Complete this business survey.

### User completes action externally

They are directed to the relevant social platform or external destination.

### User submits confirmation where required

RealReach verifies the task.

### User earns reward

Depending on campaign rules, some rewards may become available immediately and some may depend on additional requirements.

### User builds history

As the user participates, RealReach learns:

- reliability
- behaviour
- preferences
- category affinities
- saturation
- trustworthiness

### User becomes more valuable

Reliable and better-understood users can gain access to better opportunities.

---

# 34. Admin / RealReach Operator Flow

There is a third important actor:

> RealReach itself / moderators / administrators.

Particularly during the early stage, human operators may need to handle:

- campaign review
- proof review
- business disputes
- user disputes
- fraudulent accounts
- suspicious behaviour
- payout issues
- social account problems
- incorrect task submissions
- campaign refunds
- platform-policy problems

The product should not assume every decision can be fully automated from day one.

---

# 35. Fraud and Abuse Are Core Product Problems

Because RealReach pays users, abuse should be expected.

Examples include:

- multiple RealReach accounts
- multiple accounts using the same social identity
- users submitting fake proof
- users completing actions and immediately reversing them
- referral abuse
- account farming
- duplicated evidence
- users attempting to earn repeatedly from the same campaign
- artificial social accounts joining RealReach

The objective is not to assume fraud can be eliminated.

The objective is:

> Make abuse difficult enough and quality behaviour valuable enough that the economics still work.

---

# 36. Referral System

Referrals remain part of the concept.

Users may earn additional value for bringing legitimate new participants into the network.

However the product should avoid rewarding meaningless registrations.

The real objective is:

> Bring active genuine users into the marketplace.

Referral incentives should therefore ultimately be connected to meaningful participation rather than simply creating accounts.

---

# 37. Money Flow

At a conceptual level:

### Business side

Business pays RealReach to run a campaign.

### RealReach

RealReach keeps a margin/spread.

### User side

Users receive the defined reward for verified actions.

Therefore the business model remains:

> Business campaign spend  
> → RealReach  
> → user payouts + RealReach margin.

The exact economics are still to be tested.

---

# 38. Business Pricing Should Reflect Audience Quality

General users should be cheaper.

More valuable users should cost businesses more.

Example conceptually:

### General Reach

Lowest price.

### Reliable Audience

Higher.

### Interest-Matched Audience

Higher.

### Demonstrated-Interest Audience

Higher again.

### Qualified Lead

Potentially much more expensive.

This allows RealReach to monetize increasing knowledge about its network.

---

# 39. Analytics Are Potentially a Major Differentiator

A weak follower site reports:

> Delivered: 1,000.

A stronger RealReach could eventually report:

> 1,000 relevant users reached  
> 930 completed the action  
> 850 remained after 7 days  
> 790 remained after 30 days  
> 140 clicked through  
> 60 contacted the business  
> 12 purchased

RealReach may not have all these metrics initially.

The point is that over time the platform should move from:

> Quantity delivered

toward:

> Quality and outcome measured.

This is one of the clearest ways to differentiate from ordinary microtask/follower platforms.

---

# 40. Platform Policy Risk

Social networks generally dislike artificial or incentivized manipulation of engagement metrics.

The fact that RealReach users are human does NOT automatically remove all policy risk.

Therefore RealReach should avoid becoming completely dependent on:

> Artificially increase follower count.

The broader positioning around:

- paid discovery
- verified human attention
- audience research
- business exposure
- surveys
- targeted discovery
- qualified actions
- customer acquisition

creates more room for the product to evolve if any particular social-action type becomes difficult or restricted.

Follower campaigns can exist without being the company's entire identity.

---

# 41. MVP Philosophy

The current product should NOT attempt to build the most advanced theoretical RealReach before learning from real businesses.

The first version mainly needs to validate the economic loop:

> Business pays  
> → RealReach distributes campaign  
> → genuine user performs task  
> → task is verified  
> → user is rewarded  
> → business believes the result was valuable enough to buy again.

That repeat purchase is critical.

The first version does not need perfect interest inference.

It does not need perfect automation.

It does not need perfect social-platform integration.

It does not need a mature lead marketplace.

It needs to prove the core marketplace.

The developer building it is already experienced with production applications using:

- React

The web experience should support the different actors:

- earners/users

# 43. Important Product Principle: Do Not Depend on Technology That Does Not Exist

This is one of the most important development constraints.

Before relying on any external social-network capability, verify that the official platform actually supports it commercially.

RealReach should NOT assume:

- Instagram provides unrestricted follower lists
- TikTok provides unrestricted follower lists
- Facebook exposes people's entire social graphs
- any platform lets RealReach automatically manufacture followers
- every social action can be automatically verified
- every social network exposes the same information
- social APIs will remain permanently unchanged

The product should degrade gracefully when a social platform provides limited information.

# 49. Final Product Philosophy

RealReach begins with something extremely simple:

> Businesses need genuine social exposure.  
> People are willing to perform small online actions for money.

The original concept connected those two groups.

The research did not destroy that concept.

It clarified how to make it stronger.

The evolved RealReach does not merely ask:

> How do we replace bot followers with humans?

It asks:

> How do we turn a large network of real people into progressively more useful audiences for businesses?

The answer is:

- begin with genuine human actions
- learn which users are reliable
- learn what users actually care about
- prevent excessive audience saturation
- progressively improve matching
- reward better users
- charge more for better audiences
- move from follower counts toward measurable attention
- eventually move from attention toward qualified commercial intent

The platform should be built so the simple marketplace can work first while leaving room for that progression.

This is the current RealReach concept.
